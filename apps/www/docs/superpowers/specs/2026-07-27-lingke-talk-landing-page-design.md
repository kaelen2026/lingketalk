# Lingke Talk Landing Page Design

## Goal

Create a single-page personal-brand website for Lingke Talk that establishes Lingke as a thoughtful voice on technology and AI. The first release should turn interested visitors into email subscribers without requiring an existing product, publication archive, or consulting offer.

## Audience

Chinese-speaking professionals, creators, and independent thinkers who want practical, grounded perspectives on AI rather than hype.

## Positioning

Lingke Talk helps readers understand technological change while keeping human judgment at the center. The editorial promise is to explain important shifts, test useful tools, and speak with people creating the future.

## Page Structure

1. A quiet navigation bar with the Lingke Talk wordmark, links to Insights, Practice, and About, plus a subscription action.
2. A strong editorial hero with the headline “在 AI 时代，保持人的判断。” and actions to subscribe or learn about Lingke.
3. A value statement and three editorial pillars: AI insights, tool practice, and conversations.
4. An About Lingke section with a replaceable portrait area and a concise personal statement.
5. A high-contrast subscription section with an email field and clear success feedback.
6. A minimal footer with brand statement and social placeholders.

## Visual System

- Direction: future editorial newsroom rather than generic SaaS or science fiction.
- Background: warm white paper tone.
- Text: near black.
- Accent: electric blue.
- Layout: asymmetric Swiss/editorial grid, generous whitespace, thin rules, and open bands instead of card grids.
- Typography: a high-contrast Chinese serif for large editorial statements and a disciplined sans-serif for navigation, controls, and supporting copy.
- Imagery: abstract black-and-white editorial compositions. A future owner portrait can replace the About placeholder without changing layout.
- Motion: restrained entrance reveals and line movement; all motion respects reduced-motion preferences.

## Interaction

- Navigation scrolls to page sections.
- Subscription forms validate email locally and show an honest success message indicating that the first-release form is a preview until an email provider is connected.
- All links and form controls have visible hover and keyboard-focus states.
- Mobile layout stacks content in the same reading order and keeps the primary subscription action prominent.

## Content Constraints

No fabricated follower counts, testimonials, client logos, publication history, or product claims. Social links remain placeholders until Lingke provides account URLs.

## Validation

- Production build succeeds.
- Desktop and mobile layouts have no clipping or horizontal overflow.
- Hero, section order, typography, palette, spacing, and form behavior are visually checked against the approved concept images.
- Keyboard focus, form validation, success feedback, and reduced-motion behavior are verified.
